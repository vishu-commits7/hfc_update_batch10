# 4.4 Play Console declarations

Draft answers for the Data safety form, the Health Connect declaration and the subscription policy, written
from what the code does on 10 Nov 2026. I am not a lawyer; have someone who knows Indian and Play policy
read this before you submit. Each row says where in the code the answer comes from.

## 1. Data safety form

**Does the app collect or share user data?** Yes.
**Is all data encrypted in transit?** Yes (Firebase uses HTTPS; Arena video is WebRTC, which is encrypted).
**Can users ask for their data to be deleted?** Yes: Settings > Delete account (`deleteAccount` function).
Play also wants a **web link** for deletion requests. The app has none yet (see section 4).

"Collected" = leaves the phone. "Shared" = goes to another company for its own use. Google services acting
for you (Firebase) are "service providers", which Play does not count as sharing.

| Play data type | Collected | Shared | Required or optional | Why (purpose) | Where in the code |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | No | Required to use social features | App functionality, account | `users/{uid}.displayName`; shown on boards, matches, chats |
| Email address | Yes | No | Required to sign in | Account management | Firebase Auth |
| User IDs | Yes | No | Required | Account, app functionality | Firebase uid on every record |
| Photos | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality | profile picture (kept inside the user document), community posts, photo reels |
| Videos | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality | reels the user records or picks |

| Other in-app messages | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality | 1-to-1 chats (`chats/{id}/messages`). Stored on Firebase, not end-to-end encrypted |

Things that are processed but do **not** count as collected (say so if asked):
- **Camera frames for rep counting** are analysed on the phone and never leave it (`poseCounter.ts`).
- **Health Connect steps and calories** are read and shown on the phone only (`health.ts`); they are not uploaded and are not in the backup.
- **Progress photos** in the local gallery are excluded from Cloud Sync (`CLOUD_EXCLUDE`).

Two more disclosures the form does not have a row for, but your privacy policy needs:
- **Arena live video** goes straight from one player's phone to the other's (peer-to-peer). It is not stored, but the other player sees it, and a peer-to-peer link can reveal each player's IP address to the other.
- **AI workout generator**: fitness level, goal, duration, target area and equipment are sent to your own backend and on to Google's Gemini API.

## 2. Health Connect declaration

- **Permissions requested:** `READ_STEPS`, `READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED` (manifest lines 71-72; `ApexHealthPlugin.kt`). Read only. Nothing is written to Health Connect.
- **What the user gets:** today's steps and active calories and the last 7 days, shown next to their workouts.
- **Use category to pick:** Fitness and wellness.
- **Suggested wording:** "Apex Pulse reads step count and active calories burned from Health Connect to show the user their daily activity next to their workouts. The data is displayed on the device only. It is not uploaded to our servers, not included in backups, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone."
- **Required pieces that exist:** the permissions rationale activity is declared in the manifest (lines 41-53).
- **To check on a device:** the rationale screen opens from Health Connect and links to the privacy policy; turning the feature off stops all reads.

## 4. What in the code does not match what you would declare

Fix these, or change the declaration, before submitting. Most important first.

1. **The in-app Privacy Policy says "Camera feeds are never uploaded, recorded, or transmitted to any remote servers."** Not true for two features: Arena sends live video to the opponent, and reels are recorded and uploaded. Reword to: rep counting is on-device; Arena video goes directly to your opponent and is not stored; reels you post are uploaded.
2. **It says "Zero Third-Party Tracking".** The app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google). You do not sell data, which is the sentence to keep; drop "zero tracking".
3. **No public privacy policy URL.** Play requires a web address. The policy exists only as a pop-up in Settings.
4. **No web page for account deletion requests.** Play requires a link that works without installing the app. In-app deletion exists and works in code.
5. **"Optional encrypted cloud sync".** The backup is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google, like all Firestore data, but not end-to-end. Say "stored securely with Google Firebase", not "encrypted" on its own.
6. **Cloud Sync uploads body measurements** (height, weight, age) if the user entered them. That is why Health info is "Yes" above. If you would rather declare "No", strip those fields from the backup.
7. **Advertising ID.** Firebase Analytics adds the `AD_ID` permission by default, and I found no line in the manifest removing it. Either declare the advertising ID in Data safety and in the Advertising ID declaration ("Analytics"), or add `<uses-permission android:name="com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID" tools:node="remove" />`. Check the merged manifest of a release build to be sure.
8. **Microphone.** `RECORD_AUDIO` is requested for reels. The store listing and policy do not mention recording sound.
9. **No support email in the app.** The content policy and the disabled-account screen point to "the support email on our Play Store page", so that field must be filled in.
10. **Age.** The content policy says 13 or older. Set the target audience to 13+ and do not tick younger groups, or Play applies its Families rules.
11. **Chat is not end-to-end encrypted.** Do not claim it is.
12. **Terms and Privacy text are English only.**
