# 4.8 Staged rollout plan (Apex Pulse, Android)

Written 10 Nov 2026. The app has not yet run on a phone, so step 0 below comes before any percentage.

Where the numbers come from:
- Crash and ANR lines are Google Play's own "bad behaviour" thresholds (user-perceived crash rate 1.09%, ANR rate 0.47%). Above them Play can reduce the app's visibility. They are facts, not my choice.
- Rating, retention and the tighter internal targets are my suggestions. With a new app the first days have very few users, so each gate also has a minimum number of users. Change them if your real numbers show they are wrong.

Where to read each number:
- Crashes and ANRs: Play Console > Quality > Android vitals (updates about once a day), and Firebase Crashlytics (near real time).
- Rating: Play Console > Ratings and reviews (new apps show a public rating only after enough ratings).
- Retention and funnel: Firebase Analytics (events from Batch 21), Play Console > Statistics for installs and uninstalls.
- Server: Firebase Console > Functions > Logs, and Usage and billing.

## Step 0. Before any public user (internal, then closed testing)

1. Internal testing track with your own phones (up to 100 testers, no review wait). Do every item in the checklist doc.
2. Closed testing. If your Play developer account is a personal account created after 13 Nov 2023, Google requires a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row before you can apply for production. Check this in Play Console > Dashboard; it decides your earliest launch date.
3. Exit rule: 0 crashes on the main path (sign in, first workout, Arena quick match with a ghost, daily challenge, share card), rules and functions deployed, purchases tested if billing is on.

## Step 1. 5% for 3 days

Gate to move on (all must hold at the end of day 3):

| Number | Must be | Stop and fix if |
|---|---|---|
| Crash-free users (Crashlytics) | 99.0% or higher | below 98.5% on any day |
| User-perceived crash rate (Play vitals) | under 1.09% | at or above 1.09% |
| ANR rate (Play vitals) | under 0.47% | at or above 0.47% |
| Sign-in success (funnel: sign-in started to signed in) | 85% or higher (guess) | under 70% |
| First workout finished, of users who signed in | 50% or higher (guess) | under 35% |
| Function error rate (recordWorkout, arenaReport, planNext) | under 1% of calls | over 3% |
| Reviews | no repeated 1-star theme | 3 or more reviews naming the same bug |
| Minimum sample | 50 installs | fewer: stay at 5% longer instead of deciding on noise |

Day-by-day:
- Day 1: watch Crashlytics every few hours. Read every review and every private feedback message.
- Day 2: check Firestore reads against COSTS.md (reads per daily active user). If reads are more than double the estimate, find the screen before raising the percentage.
- Day 3: decide.

## Step 2. 20% for 4 days

| Number | Must be | Stop and fix if |
|---|---|---|
| Crash-free users | 99.3% or higher | below 99.0% |
| User-perceived crash rate | under 0.8% (my target; Play's limit is 1.09%) | at or above 1.09% |
| ANR rate | under 0.3% (my target; Play's limit is 0.47%) | at or above 0.47% |
| Day-1 retention (came back the next day) | 30% or higher (guess; fitness apps are often 20 to 35%) | under 20% |
| Day-3 retention | 15% or higher (guess) | under 10% |
| Average rating | 4.2 or higher once there are 20+ ratings | under 3.8 |
| Uninstalls on day 0 | under 35% of installs (guess) | over 50% |
| Push opt-in | no gate, just record it | |
| Cost | within 2x of COSTS.md per daily active user | more than 3x |
| Minimum sample | 200 installs | fewer: extend this step |

Also at this step: confirm the Monday league rollover ran (function log), the daily challenge changed at midnight IST, and ghost matches are being found once enough matches exist.

## Step 3. 100%

Move to 100% only when Step 2 held for 4 full days. After that, for the first two weeks check daily:
- Crash rate and ANR stay under Play's limits.
- Day-7 retention 8% or higher (guess). Under 5% means the habit loop (streak, quests, league) is not landing; look at which Today-card action people take.
- Rating 4.2 or higher.
- Moderation queue checked at least once a day (Settings > Moderation). Flagged accounts reviewed.
- Billing, if on: purchase success, `verifyPurchase` errors, refunds.

## If a gate fails

1. Halt the rollout: Play Console > Production > Releases > "Halt rollout". New users stop getting this version; people who already have it keep it. There is no way to pull a version back off phones.
2. Decide which kind of problem it is:
   - Server-side (a function, a rule, an index): fix and deploy the function or rule. No new app build, takes minutes. Most Arena, XP, league and daily-challenge logic is server-side, so many fixes are this kind.
   - A feature is broken but the rest is fine: turn it off without a new build where a switch exists: `BILLING_ENABLED=false` (functions/.env, redeploy functions), the sign-in gate flag (Batch 28). Arena, reels and community have no remote switch today; that is a gap (see 4.9).
   - App-side crash: fix, raise `versionCode`, build, upload, and start again at 5%. Do not resume the halted release.
3. Tell users when it matters: reply to the reviews that reported it once the fix is live.
4. Write one line in BUGFIX_NOTES.md: what failed, the number, what fixed it.

## Things that would make me stop regardless of the numbers

- Any report of one user seeing another user's private data.
- XP, rating or purchases granted twice or to the wrong account.
- A moderation report that cannot be actioned from the admin screen.
- A Play policy warning email. Fix that first; warnings have deadlines.
