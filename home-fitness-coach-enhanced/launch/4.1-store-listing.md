# 4.1 Play Store listing copy

Written from what the app does today (10 Nov 2026). Lengths are counted by script. The Hindi is my translation and needs a native speaker before you publish it.

## Check before publishing

- **Programs:** the app has 2 full programs written, both marked as drafts for a trainer. The copy says "multi-week programs"; do not add a number until more exist and a trainer has approved them.
- **Pro:** billing is still switched off (`BILLING_ENABLED`). Remove the Pro sentence if you launch without it.
- **Hindi in the app:** only the main screens are translated. "English and Hindi" is true, "fully in Hindi" would not be.
- **Voice coaching** speaks English only.
- No weight-loss numbers, medical claims, or "best/#1" claims are used, on purpose: Play rejects them.

```
Get a workout you can start in seconds, at home, with no equipment. Apex Pulse plans today's session for you, shows you every move, and gives you a reason to come back tomorrow.

WHY PEOPLE STICK WITH IT
• No guessing. Open the app and today's workout is ready, matched to your goal, level and the days you train.

• Works without a connection. Finish your session offline and it syncs when you are back online.

TRAIN WITH OTHER PEOPLE
• Arena: a live 1v1 against a friend or another player. Same exercise, first to the target wins.
• Nobody online? Race a ghost: the recorded run of a real player near your level, or your own best.
• Camera rep counting for push-ups, squats, lunges and sit-ups. Tap counting for everything else.
• One daily challenge for everyone, with a top 100 board that resets at midnight IST.
• Weekly leagues: earn XP, finish in the top 5 of your group and move up.
• Crews, friend challenges and a feed to share your progress.

MADE FOR HOW YOU TRAIN
• Multi-week programs you can follow step by step.
• Optional voice coaching that counts you in and calls the next move.
• Levels, ranks and trophies for the work you actually do.
• English and Hindi.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Free to use. Arena and the social features stay free. An optional Pro subscription adds extras such as the reason behind each day's plan and a 3-day outlook.
• Apex Pulse is a fitness app, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, and stop if something hurts.
• You can report and block anyone, and delete your account and data from Settings.
```

### What to ask the Hindi reviewer

- Are the loan words natural (वर्कआउट, स्ट्रीक, एरीना, घोस्ट, लीग) or should any be translated?
- Title 2 mixes Hindi and "1v1"; is that fine for the store?
- I avoided the transliteration "रेप" for "rep" everywhere because it reads as an offensive word in Hindi; "गिनती" (count) and "बार" (times) are used instead. Confirm these read naturally.
